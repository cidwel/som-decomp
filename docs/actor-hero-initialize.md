# Hero initialization

C00039 → C03821 → C04D72 initializes the three aligned party slots (0000, 0200, 0400). It clears the identity/attribute record, chooses identity 80/81/82 from the slot, loads level-zero attributes with controller/personality adjustments, fills current HP/MP from maximum HP/MP, and sets the initial equipment from C04E2B's three near pointers.

The four initial equipment bytes supply three inventory items (zero entries skipped) and a weapon item offset by 80. C06423 reaches the existing C064B3 producer with owner 1/2/3. Inventory insertion follows its real category, capacity and controller-bit rules, then the initializer rebuilds full actor attributes through C04530. The saved processor status, bank and direct page are restored by the real return sequence.

Existing C04E31, C0464F, C064B3/C064BD/C064D1/C0645C and C04530 sources are reused without duplicate byte credit. The new sources are the 185-byte initializer, three 3-byte public jumps and the 97-byte C7507F entered-name callback (291 instruction bytes). The callback clears the selected 12-byte name slot, translates 1–12 entered glyph tiles through the original C774B2 table, stores the name, and invokes the complete initializer for the selected hero. Six typed source bytes reconstruct the three equipment pointers (57BD, 57C1, 57C5).

Validation: smoke three calls and then 51 complete original calls both returned successfully on the first comparison. The full batch compares randomized 128 KiB WRAM outside the observed native stack, full CPU result and restored context, and all static peripherals. It covers all three heroes, all public entries, empty/full inventories, bound/unbound controllers, both accumulator widths and name lengths 1/6/12. There are no bypass hooks, patched ROM records or persisted ROM/name/graphics payloads.

Execution coverage observes 129 of the 133 instruction starts.
