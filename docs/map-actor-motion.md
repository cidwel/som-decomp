# Live map actor movement

Three complete bodies contribute 226 matching instruction bytes: C0D1FE's 43-byte actor-pool dispatcher, C0D229's 153-byte live actor mover, and C0D2C2's 30-byte vertical helper. All109 own instruction starts and every immediate M/X width are observed in passing original comparisons, and the three readable assembly sources independently assemble exactly to the ROM. Shared helper bodies receive no new source credit here.

The pool skips boss mode and scans aligned actor offsets 0600..1800, or 0600..0C00 when ED.N is set. It preserves each actor offset across the near movement call. The mover returns for inactive or negative-status actors, decrements a live movement delay, and restores an expired delay from E036. It optionally updates E045 from E008, clamps signed vertical motion, copies velocity and layer geometry to direct page, applies contact flags and ground effects, queries the actual terrain, and publishes accepted wrapped positions and layer/priority fields. The vertical helper uses the incoming N flag to choose its positive/negative clamp; it does not recompute the incoming sign.

`tools/map_actor_motion.py` models the entire own mover and pool bodies. Its shared helper contracts cover the NPC domain of D2E0/D482, D74F and D890. They implement contact-imposed motion, oscillating ground, signed currents, presentation-volume changes, ground-triggered event dispatch, terrain rejection/acceptance, layer switching, and toroidal coordinate publication. Terrain testing calls the existing frozen `ActorMapTerrainQueries.point_step` for the real JSL01B93E. These helpers reject entry into unimplemented hero-specific domains; no return or successful result substitutes for an original body. Active hero movement and complete shared-helper source recovery remain separate.

Primary28 passes 28 complete original calls and 8,859 instructions, including a real C0D5C0 caller with an active NPC, pool dispatch, both layers, signed vertical clamps, delay expiry, blocked and skipped terrain, and ground effects20/30/40/D0. Supplemental floor3 passes three further original mover calls and 1,035 instructions on actual ROM-produced map16 cells. The accepted positions are (296,376)→(297,376), (856,520)→(857,520) with published priority7, and (408,344)→(408,345). Frozen caller6 evidence supplies the pool's other bound and boss guard, without adding its already-counted45 bytes again. The combined own-source audit uses 37 calls and 11,511 original instructions; 31 calls and 9,894 instructions are newly captured here.

Terrain and attributes come from the existing ROM map producer. Aligned actor fields, velocities and selected ground effects remain explicit actor-body ABI inputs. The original native floor cells establish accepted terrain and priority publication without rewriting their map attributes. This proof does not establish every actor producer or a full frame scheduler. Hero movement, continuous gameplay, camera response and cycle timing remain open.

Every comparison checks WRAM outside the measured native stack, SRAM, logical CPU/context, peripherals and ordered audio. No original instruction is bypassed by a hook, no ROM is patched, and no ROM/map payload is saved. Existing contacts, terrain models and caller6 proof dependency hashes are preserved. Evidence lives in `data/map_actor_motion_proof.json`, `data/map_actor_motion_floor.json` and their separate captures; `data/map_actor_motion_source_audit.json` records the source and proof/capture hashes and exact assemblies.
