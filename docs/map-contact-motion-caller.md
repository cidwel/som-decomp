# Contact-before-motion caller

C0D5C0..C0D5EC contains 45 instruction bytes and 21 instruction starts. The recovered caller first checks F1.V, returning immediately when set. Otherwise it saves DB, selects DB7E, calls the ordinary contacts routine through its real low-bank JSL01CD4E, dispatches D5ED for hero offsets 0000/0200/0400, calls the D1FE map-actor pool, and restores DB. Each hero dispatch explicitly selects X16; the guard returns preserve the incoming X width.

The readable model is `tools/map_contact_motion_caller.py`. The contacts dependency is the frozen `LiveMapContacts` adapter and its existing models. The downstream motion entry contracts are deliberately limited to the original inactive, inhibited and nonexpired-delay paths: D5ED checks E00F before activity, D229 checks activity, and both can decrement a nonexpired E037 while E036 is set. These guards run their actual native bodies during the original comparison. The model rejects an active movement path outside this contract rather than substituting a successful return. No motion callee receives new source credit here.

Six complete original caller comparisons pass, executing 1,617 instructions. They cover F1.V pause; empty ordinary and terrain pools; a positive contact with the actual map15 record1 NPC created through the frozen Creation producer; an active hero held by its native nonexpired delay; and the boss actor-pool guard. All21 own starts and unique immediate M/X widths are observed. The caller source independently assembles exactly to the ROM. WRAM outside the measured native stack, SRAM, logical CPU/context, peripherals and ordered audio match. No hooks, ROM changes or successful-result substitution are used.

Map terrain and the native NPC/event1401 are produced by the existing frozen fixtures. Hero input positions, motion inhibition and delay fields are explicit caller ABI inputs; this proof does not establish a gameplay producer for those guard states. Both initial M widths and X widths are exercised, with D0, binary arithmetic and incoming DB0; the caller establishes DB7E and restores DB0 on its live path.

Earlier `tools/hero_movement.py`, `tools/map_collision.py` and `tools/map_layers.py` document useful original movement observations. Their selected frame-wait or party-window hooks and save-state fixtures do not satisfy this independent complete caller comparison contract, and none of their movement bytes or experiments is counted again. Full D5ED/D229 movement, player input, continuous frames, scheduler context and cycle timing remain separate work.

Evidence is saved in `data/map_contact_motion_caller_proof.json` and its own coverage capture. `data/map_contact_motion_caller_source_audit.json` verifies frozen dependency identities, own starts/widths and exact assembly, preserving proof/capture and source hashes. The only proposed source addition is C0D5C0's 45-byte prefix; contacts retain their existing 1,855-byte credit.
